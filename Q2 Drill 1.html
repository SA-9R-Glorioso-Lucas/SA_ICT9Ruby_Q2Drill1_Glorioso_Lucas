Guides on Badminton



Badminton is a racket sport played by either two opposing players (singles) or two opposing pairs (doubles), who take positions on opposite halves of a rectangular court that is divided by a net.

The Rules
  • In order to score, the shuttlecock must be at the opponent's side of the court.
  • To serve, it should be hit below waist level.
  • For winning, the teams should be able to score 21 points.
Differences of the Shuttlecocks
  • Feather: This one is used more in tournaments, performance, and professional play.
  • Plastic: This one is better for durability, budget, and best for beginners. It's just not for professional play.
Basic Equipments
  • Badminton Racket
  • Feather/Plastic Shuttlecock
  • Sports Clothing
  • Good Shoes
